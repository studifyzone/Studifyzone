FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with Studify Zone.

Do you use ChatGPT or AI tools?

▾
Absolutely not. Every line is written from scratch using academic books, trusted peer-reviewed libraries, and reliable sources. No AI is involved at any stage of the writing process — and your Turnitin report confirms this.

What subjects can you help with?

▾
We provide support for Business, Healthcare, Nursing, IT, Marketing, HRM, Finance, Law, Engineering, and many more subjects.

Can you send me the final assignment first, and I'll pay later?

▾
To keep our writers allocated to your project, a confirmation deposit is required. However, we offer low token deposits (£10–£15) and will happily send you progress screenshots to verify our work and quality before final delivery.

What if my university report shows a high similarity index?

▾
Matches on things like references, citations, or the table of contents are completely standard and ignored by universities. However, if any core content needs tweaking, we will revise and reduce it for you immediately — free of charge, within our priority rework window.

How quickly can you complete my assignment?

▾
We handle both standard and urgent deadlines. The turnaround depends on word count and complexity, but we regularly deliver within 24–72 hours for urgent requests. Contact us with your deadline and we'll confirm immediately.

Is my personal information kept private?

▾
Absolutely. We operate with full confidentiality. Your name, university, and details are never shared with any third party. Our communication channels (WhatsApp and email) are private, and we delete sensitive data after project completion upon request.
