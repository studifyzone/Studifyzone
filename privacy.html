1. Privacy Policy

Studify Zone ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the personal information of every student and visitor who interacts with our services. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your information.

1.1 Information We Collect

  • Name and contact number (provided voluntarily when you reach out)
  • Assignment brief, instructions, or academic documents you share with us
  • University name or course details you choose to disclose
  • Payment confirmation references (we do not store card details)
  • WhatsApp message history related to your order

1.2 How We Use Your Information

  • To process, assign, and deliver your requested academic assistance service
  • To send order updates, progress screenshots, and delivery notifications
  • To respond to your support queries or revision requests
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

1.3 Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as is necessary to complete your order and provide after-delivery support. Upon your written request, we will permanently delete your personal data from our records within 14 business days.

1.4 Data Security

All communications are conducted through encrypted channels (WhatsApp end-to-end encryption and secure email). We do not store assignment files on public servers. Access to your data is restricted to the assigned team member handling your order.

We never sell, rent, share, or disclose your personal information to any third party, advertiser, or data broker under any circumstances.

1.5 GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, port, or erase your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at studifyzoneuk@gmail.com.

2. WhatsApp Messaging Policy

Studify Zone uses WhatsApp as a primary communication channel for student support and order management. The following terms govern how we use WhatsApp to contact you.

2.1 Opt-In Consent

By initiating a conversation with Studify Zone via WhatsApp, or by submitting your phone number through our website contact channels, you expressly consent to receive the following types of messages:

  • Order confirmation and booking acknowledgement messages
  • Assignment progress updates and progress screenshot notifications
  • Delivery notifications when your completed work is ready
  • Post-delivery support messages regarding revisions or feedback
  • Payment reminders related to your active order

2.2 Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your order status and activity. During active orders, you may receive between 2–8 messages per day. Once an order is completed, messages will cease unless you initiate further contact.

2.3 No Marketing Messages

We do not send unsolicited promotional or marketing messages via WhatsApp. All messages sent are directly related to your enquiry or active order with Studify Zone.

2.4 Carrier Rates

Standard message and data rates charged by your mobile carrier may apply when receiving or sending WhatsApp messages. Studify Zone is not responsible for any such carrier charges.

Your WhatsApp conversations with Studify Zone are private, end-to-end encrypted, and are never shared with third parties.

3. Opt-Out Instructions

You have the right to withdraw your consent to receive WhatsApp messages from Studify Zone at any time. To opt out:

  • Reply STOP to any WhatsApp message you receive from us
  • Send an email to studifyzoneuk@gmail.com with the subject line "Opt-Out Request"
  • Contact our HR team at rumanastudifyzone@gmail.com

Upon receipt of your opt-out request, we will remove you from all active communication lists within 48 hours. Please note that opting out may prevent us from completing or supporting your active order.

To opt out, simply reply STOP to any message, or email studifyzoneuk@gmail.com. We will action your request within 48 hours.

4. Terms of Service

4.1 Service Description

Studify Zone provides academic tutoring, research assistance, and educational support services to students. All materials produced are intended for use as reference, study aids, and learning tools only.

4.2 Payment Terms

  • A deposit of 50% of the agreed fee, or a booking token of £10–£15, is required before work commences.
  • The remaining balance is due upon delivery and the client's satisfaction with the completed work.
  • All pricing is quoted on a £15–£25 per 1,000 words basis, scaled by complexity, academic level, and deadline.
  • Payments are non-refundable once the final completed work has been delivered and accepted.

4.3 Delivery & Revisions

  • Delivery timelines are agreed upon at the time of booking and confirmed via WhatsApp.
  • Free revisions are offered within a 24–72 hour priority window from the date of delivery.
  • Revision requests must be consistent with the original brief submitted at the time of booking.

4.4 Limitation of Liability

Studify Zone provides educational support services and cannot guarantee specific academic outcomes or grades. All work is produced to the highest standard of research and quality. The client assumes full responsibility for how the delivered material is used.

4.5 Acceptable Use

Clients agree to use all materials provided by Studify Zone strictly in accordance with their institution's academic policies and our Fair Use Policy outlined below.

5. Fair Use Policy

By engaging our services, you confirm that you understand and agree to use all delivered materials ethically and responsibly. Studify Zone does not condone, encourage, or facilitate any form of academic dishonesty or misconduct.
